Workspaces

A workspace is a self-contained space: its own connected social accounts, instructions, runs and approvals. Nothing is shared between workspaces, so an account connected in one cannot be posted to from another. Every workspace is private to you until you add a member — and any of them can be shared, including the one you started with.
