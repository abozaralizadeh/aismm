Deleting your data

Last updated {{ updated }}.

If you connected an Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X, YouTube, LinkedIn or Reddit account to {{ product_name }}, you can have everything we store about that account deleted at any time.

What is deleted

  • The connection itself: the access tokens that let the Service act for you (stored encrypted), your account name and ID, and the settings kept with it.
  • The history of what the Service did on that account: captions, links to published posts, the statistics it read about them, and its logs of each run.
  • Anything waiting for approval: drafted posts and replies, including any quoted comments or messages they answer.

Posts that were already published stay on the platform, because they are yours there: delete them in the platform's own app. Server logs are size-limited and are overwritten as they rotate.

How to ask

Instagram and Facebook

These accounts connect through Facebook Login, so the request starts in your Facebook settings: open Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, find {{ product_name }}, remove it, and ask for your data to be deleted. Facebook tells us automatically, we delete your data straight away, and Facebook shows you a confirmation code that you can check below.

TikTok

In TikTok, open Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services and remove {{ product_name }}. TikTok tells us, and we delete the data of that account automatically.

X, YouTube, LinkedIn and Reddit

These platforms do not tell apps when you remove them, so please do both of these:

  1. Remove the Service's access: X · Google (YouTube) · LinkedIn · Reddit.
  2. {% if contact_email %}Email {{ contact_email }}{% else %}Contact the operator of this service{% endif %} with the platform and the account name. We delete the data within 30 days and reply with a confirmation code.

Any platform, by email

You can always ask by email instead{% if contact_email %}: {{ contact_email }}{% endif %}. Tell us the platform and the account name.
