Privacy Policy

Last updated {{ updated }}.

This Policy explains how {{ product_name }} (the “Service”), operated by {{ entity_name }} (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use it to publish to third-party social media platforms.

1. Information we process

  • Account identity. When you sign in (where single sign-on is enabled), we store your email address and display name to identify you and record when you last signed in and were active.
  • Connected-platform tokens. When you connect a social account, the platform’s authorization (OAuth) access and refresh tokens are stored so the Service can publish on your behalf. Tokens are encrypted at rest and are never displayed back to you or written to logs in plain text.
  • Content and configuration. The instructions, captions, generated media, schedules, and run history you create are stored to operate the Service.
  • Platform data. To publish and to respond to engagement, the Service reads and writes data through platform APIs — for example posts, comments, mentions, and direct messages on the accounts you connect — strictly to carry out the actions you configure.
  • Operational logs. Standard technical logs (timestamps, errors, run outcomes) are kept to run and troubleshoot the Service.

2. How we use information

We use the information only to provide the Service: to authenticate you, connect your accounts, generate and publish content you have configured, respond to engagement where enabled, show you run history and results, and keep the Service secure and working. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your private content or messages for advertising.

3. AI processing

To generate media and text, the Service sends the prompts and reference material you provide to AI providers configured for your deployment. Those providers process the request to return a result and are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Sharing

Information is shared only with: the social platforms you connect (to publish and engage as you direct); the AI and infrastructure providers configured for your deployment (to generate content and store data); and where required by law. Within the Service, data is scoped to your workspace and its members.

5. Retention

We keep information for as long as your account and connections are active. Generated media may be pruned from local storage on a retention schedule once safely archived. When you disconnect an account its stored tokens are removed, and you can request deletion of your data.

6. Security

Access tokens and other secrets are encrypted at rest, access to the dashboard is protected by sign-in where configured, and media links are unguessable. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

7. Your choices and rights

You can disconnect any platform account at any time, edit or delete your instructions and content, and request access to or deletion of your personal information. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA; contact us to exercise them.

8. Revoking platform access

Besides disconnecting inside the Service, you can revoke the Service’s access directly from each platform’s own app or account settings (for example, the connected-apps or security section of Instagram, X, YouTube, TikTok, or Reddit).

9. Children

The Service is not directed to children and is intended for users old enough to hold accounts on the connected platforms.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy; the “last updated” date above reflects the current version. Material changes will be reflected here.
